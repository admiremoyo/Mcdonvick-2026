Tax calendar

Never miss a ZIMRA deadline.

Late returns and payments attract penalties and interest. These are the key dates most Zimbabwean businesses work to. We track them for every client.

  • 10th monthly PAYE & NSSA for the previous month
  • 25th monthly VAT return & payment
  • QPDs 25 Mar (10%) · 25 Jun (25%) · 25 Sep (30%) · 20 Dec (35%)

FAQ

Questions we hear often.

Can't find your answer? Send us a message and a qualified accountant will reply within 24 hours.

Ask us anything
Do you work with small businesses and start-ups?

Yes. A large share of our clients are SMEs and new businesses. We can register your company, set up your books and tax registrations, and grow our support as you grow.

How much do your services cost?

It depends on the size of your business and the services you need. After a free consultation we give you a fixed monthly or once-off fee in writing, with no hidden extras.

I'm behind on my ZIMRA returns. Can you help?

Yes. We regularly help businesses bring overdue returns up to date, work out what is owed and engage ZIMRA on their behalf, including on payment arrangements where possible.

Can you help me get a tax clearance certificate?

Yes. We check that your returns and payments are in order, fix any gaps and apply for your tax clearance (ITF263), which you often need for tenders and contracts.

Do I need to come to your office?

No. Most clients share documents by email or WhatsApp and we meet online when needed. You're always welcome at our office at 37 Lawson Avenue, Milton Park.

Which accounting software do you use?

We work extensively with Pastel (Sage) and can work with the system you already use, or recommend one that fits your business.
