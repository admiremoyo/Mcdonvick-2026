Service

Accounting & Bookkeeping

Accurate, up-to-date books and financial statements you can rely on: for ZIMRA, for your bank and investors, and for your own decisions.

Book a free consultation WhatsApp us

Good decisions start with good numbers. We keep your books current every month, reconcile every account and turn the figures into clear reports that show how your business is really doing.

Whether you need a monthly bookkeeping routine, a set of annual financial statements for the bank, or help clearing years of backlog before an audit, our team handles it properly and on time.

What's included

Monthly bookkeeping

Sales, purchases, expenses and receipts captured and categorised every month, so your records are always current.

Reconciliations

Bank, cash, supplier and customer accounts reconciled monthly, so errors and missing payments are caught early.

Management accounts

Monthly or quarterly profit and loss, balance sheet and cash-flow reports with plain-language commentary.

Annual financial statements

Year-end financial statements prepared in line with International Financial Reporting Standards, ready for ZIMRA, banks and shareholders.

Audit preparation

Schedules, reconciliations and supporting documents prepared for your external auditors, plus liaison while the audit is underway.

Backlog clean-up

Months or years behind? We rebuild your records from bank statements and source documents and bring everything up to date.

Multi-currency records

US$ and ZiG transactions recorded and reported correctly, so your books match how your business actually trades.

Pastel (Sage) support

Pastel setup, chart of accounts, month-end routines, troubleshooting and regular data backups.

Who it's for

  • Small and medium businesses without an in-house accountant
  • Growing companies that need monthly management information
  • Businesses applying for bank finance or preparing for an audit
  • Owners who are behind on their records and need a clean start

What we'll need

  • Bank statements for all business accounts
  • Sales invoices and receipts
  • Supplier invoices and expense receipts
  • Payroll records (if you have staff)
  • Last year's financial statements, if available
  • Access to your accounting software, if you use one

Common questions

How often do you need my documents?

Monthly works best. We send you a short checklist and you can share everything by email or WhatsApp, with no need to visit our office.

I'm a year or more behind. Can you still help?

Yes. Backlog work is common. We start from your bank statements, rebuild the records and bring your books and tax filings up to date in a planned order.

Do you carry out audits?

We prepare you for audit: reconciled accounts, schedules and supporting documents. We then work alongside your appointed external auditors so the audit runs smoothly.

Which accounting software do you use?

We work extensively with Pastel (Sage) and can work with the system you already use, or recommend one that suits your business.
