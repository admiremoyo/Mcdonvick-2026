Business Consultancy

Numbers that help you plan, raise finance and grow, not just report on the past.

Book a free consultation WhatsApp us

Banks, investors and funders want to see that you understand your numbers. Owners need to know where the business is heading before cash runs short.

We turn your figures into practical plans: business plans and projections that stand up to scrutiny, budgets you can manage against, and clear strategies when a business needs to turn around.

What's included

Business plans

Bank-ready and investor-ready business plans and funding proposals, with the financials to support them.

Projections & cash-flow forecasts

Three- to five-year financial projections and monthly cash-flow forecasts built on realistic assumptions.

Budgets & variance analysis

Annual budgets and monthly comparisons of actual against budget, so you can act early.

Feasibility studies

Test a new product, branch or project before you commit capital to it.

Turnaround strategies

Cost, pricing and cash-flow reviews with a practical plan for businesses under pressure.

Costing & pricing

Understand the true cost of your products and services and price them for profit.

Corporate governance

Board structures, policies and financial controls that protect the business and build trust.

Tender support

Financial sections and compliance documents, including tax clearance, prepared for tender submissions.

Who it's for

  • Start-ups raising their first finance
  • Established businesses planning expansion
  • NGOs and projects preparing funding proposals
  • Businesses facing cash-flow pressure

What we'll need

  • A description of the business, project or idea
  • Historical financial statements (for existing businesses)
  • Key assumptions: prices, volumes, costs and staffing
  • Any lender or funder requirements and templates

Common questions

Will a business plan guarantee a loan?

No one can guarantee a lending decision, but a well-prepared plan with credible projections gives you the best possible chance and answers the questions lenders ask.

Can you help with tenders?

Yes. We prepare the financial information and compliance documents tenders require and check your tax clearance and company records are current.

How long does a business plan take?

It depends on the size of the project and how quickly we receive your information. We agree a timeline with you at the start.
