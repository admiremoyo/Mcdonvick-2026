Company Secretarial

Register your business correctly and keep it in good standing with the Registrar of Companies, year after year.

Book a free consultation WhatsApp us

The Companies and Other Business Entities (COBE) Act sets out how Zimbabwean companies and Private Business Corporations are formed and maintained. Missing filings can leave your company non-compliant just when you need it most, for a tender, a loan or a sale.

We handle the paperwork from name search to certificate of incorporation, then keep your statutory records, returns and changes up to date.

What's included

Name search & reservation

Name search with the Registrar. Approved names are reserved for up to 30 days while registration is completed.

Company & PBC registration

Private Limited Company or Private Business Corporation registration under the COBE Act, through to your certificate of incorporation.

Post-registration setup

ZIMRA registration (TIN), NSSA employer registration and the documents your bank needs to open an account.

Annual returns

Annual returns and statutory filings with the Registrar so your company stays in good standing.

Statutory changes

Changes of directors, members, shareholders and registered office, including CR6 and CR11 filings.

Registers & minutes

Statutory registers, resolutions and minutes prepared and kept up to date.

Share transfers

Share and member interest transfers documented and filed correctly.

Company shelving

Dormant company shelving and deregistration when a business is no longer trading.

Who it's for

  • Entrepreneurs starting a new business
  • Sole traders ready to formalise as a company or PBC
  • Companies with overdue returns or out-of-date records
  • Businesses changing ownership, directors or address

What we'll need

  • Five proposed company names, in order of preference
  • Copies of IDs or passports for all directors or members
  • Proof of residence for directors or members
  • The business's physical address
  • The ownership split between shareholders or members

Common questions

Should I register a PBC or a Private Limited Company?

A PBC suits owner-run businesses with 1 to 20 members who manage the business themselves. A Private Limited Company (up to 50 shareholders, with directors and a company secretary) is better if you plan to raise investment or bid for larger tenders. Our guide compares them in detail.

How long does registration take?

It depends mainly on the Registrar's turnaround at the time. We'll give you a realistic estimate when you instruct us, and keep you updated at each stage.

Do I need to register with ZIMRA after incorporation?

Yes. Every company needs a TIN, and you may also need VAT and PAYE registration. We can handle this straight after incorporation.

My company hasn't filed returns for years. Can it be fixed?

Usually, yes. We review the company's status with the Registrar and file the outstanding returns to restore good standing.
