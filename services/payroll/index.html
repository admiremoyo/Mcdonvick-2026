Payroll Administration

Your staff paid correctly and on time, with every statutory deduction calculated, submitted and recorded, every month.

Book a free consultation WhatsApp us

Payroll mistakes are costly: unhappy staff, ZIMRA penalties and NSSA arrears. Getting PAYE right is harder when salaries are paid in US$, ZiG or both.

We run your payroll every month, apply the correct tax tables, prepare payslips and submit PAYE, NSSA and ZIMDEF on time. Your payroll information stays strictly confidential.

What's included

Monthly payroll processing

Salaries, allowances, overtime, benefits and deductions calculated accurately every pay period.

PAYE & AIDS levy

PAYE worked out with the correct US$ or ZiG tax tables and remitted to ZIMRA by the 10th of the following month.

NSSA compliance

Employer and employee NSSA registration, monthly P4 returns and contributions submitted on time.

ZIMDEF levy

Standards development (ZIMDEF) levy calculated on your payroll and paid on schedule.

Payslips

Clear, professional payslips for every employee, delivered by email or printed.

Terminations & final pay

Final salary, leave pay and tax on termination packages calculated correctly.

Year-end certificates

Employee tax certificates and the employer's annual PAYE return prepared at year end.

Leave & payroll records

Leave balances and payroll history kept organised and ready for NSSA or ZIMRA inspection.

Who it's for

  • Employers of any size, from a single employee upwards
  • Employers paying salaries in US$, ZiG or a mix
  • Owners who want payroll kept confidential from internal staff
  • Companies with NSSA or PAYE arrears to regularise

What we'll need

  • Employee list with ID numbers, start dates and salaries
  • Allowances, overtime and deductions for the month
  • Your ZIMRA (TIN) and NSSA employer numbers
  • Leave taken and any new starters or leavers

Common questions

When are PAYE and NSSA due?

PAYE must reach ZIMRA by the 10th of the month after payment. NSSA contributions and the P4 return are also generally due by the 10th of the following month. We handle both for you.

We pay some staff in US$ and some in ZiG. Is that a problem?

Not at all. We apply the correct tax tables for each currency and keep your payroll records and returns consistent.

Is there a minimum number of employees?

No. We run payroll for businesses with one employee as well as larger teams.

Who sees our salary information?

Only the McDonvick staff handling your payroll. We treat all client information as strictly confidential.
