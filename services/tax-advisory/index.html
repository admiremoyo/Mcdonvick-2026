Tax Advisory & ZIMRA

Stay fully compliant with ZIMRA and pay no more tax than the law requires. We handle registrations, returns and correspondence on your behalf.

We take that burden off you. From your first TIN to QPDs, VAT returns and ZIMRA audits, we keep you compliant, plan ahead to reduce your tax legally, and deal with ZIMRA so you don't have to.

Common questions

What is TaRMS?

TaRMS (Tax and Revenue Management System) is ZIMRA's online platform for registrations, returns, payments and tax clearance. Taxpayers must claim their TIN and register on its self-service portal. We can do this with you.

Do I need to register for VAT?

Registration is compulsory once your taxable supplies exceed, or are expected to exceed, US$25,000 (or the ZiG equivalent) in 12 months. Businesses below the threshold can register voluntarily. See our VAT registration guide for details.

How do I get a tax clearance certificate?

Under TaRMS, tax clearance is issued to taxpayers who are up to date with all returns and payments. If yours isn't coming through, there is usually an outstanding return or balance, which we can find and fix.

ZIMRA has contacted me. What should I do?

Don't ignore it, and don't reply before getting advice. Send us the letter or message and we'll explain what it means and handle the response.
